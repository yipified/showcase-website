About Levinia

The Empire of Levinia is a micronation with claims in South Carolina and New York. Its capital is the city-state of Imperia, though the true seat of government is wherever the Sovereign resides. Levinia is an anocratic absolute monarchy under Emperor Benjamin I, "The Great Founder," advised by a democratically elected Parliament and a Council of Ministers.
